Working Without Internet

What the weighbridge can and cannot do while the connection is down

Document v1.1  ·  19 July 2026  ·  Prepared for sites with an intermittent link  ·  Runs in Google Chrome (installed app)

The system is cloud-hosted, so a dropped connection normally stops work. For sites where the link fails for 30–40 minutes at a time, we are making the weighbridge keep operating through the outage and sync automatically afterwards. This sheet states plainly what works today, what is being built, and what will always need a connection.

1Works without internet — today

FeatureStatusWhat actually happens
The app stays open Live now The screen does not go blank. The page remains usable for the whole outage.
Live weight from the scale Live now The screen reads the weighbridge indicator directly on the plant PC, bypassing the internet entirely. The weight keeps moving and the Capture button still works. An amber Reading scale directly — will sync later badge appears so the operator knows.
Requires the updated scale agent — see the note below.
Recording a new trip Live now Vehicle, customer, material and remarks are saved on the PC and upload automatically when the line returns. The operator sees “Saved offline — will sync when the connection returns”.
Nothing is ever lost Live now A trip saved offline is never discarded. If the server later refuses it, it is held and flagged for a person to resolve — it is not silently deleted.
Outage is visible Live now The header shows Offline with a count of items waiting, and the system actively checks the server rather than trusting Windows’ network icon — which reports “connected” even when a 4G dongle has no working link.
Two one-time setup steps on the weighbridge PC 1. Install the app in Chrome. On the weighbridge PC, open the site in Google Chrome and choose Install (the install icon in the address bar). It then runs as its own app with a desktop icon, keeps its pages ready without depending on a browser tab, and its storage is protected by Windows. Chrome is the supported browser for the plant PC.
2. Install the updated scale agent. Live weight during an outage needs the updated scale agent on the PC. Until that is done the weight display goes blank when the connection drops, even though everything else here works. A five-minute, one-time install.

2Current limitations — please read

Numbers are issued on sync A trip recorded during an outage receives its token and gate-pass number when the connection returns, not at the moment of weighing. A printed slip carrying the final number is therefore not available during the outage. (Being addressed — Section 3.)
Do not reload or close the browser Customer, material and vehicle lists are not yet stored on the PC. If the page is reloaded or the browser closed mid-outage, those dropdowns will be empty and a new trip cannot be started until the connection returns. Leave the tab open. (Being addressed — Section 3.)
Second weighment Closing out a truck that is already on the bridge still needs the connection today. (Being addressed — Section 3.)
Printing Weight slips and invoices are produced by the server, so printing needs the connection.

3Being built — full offline operation

A small database is being added to the weighbridge PC itself. The plant then keeps working normally through an outage, and everything uploads automatically once the line returns. The office system remains the single source of record.

CapabilityStatusResult for the operator
Gate pass at entry In build Issued offline with a real number the driver can be given.
Both weighments In build First and second weight captured offline; a truck can be fully completed.
Customer / material lists In build Held on the PC, so the form works even after a reload or a restart.
Printing the weight slip In build Printed on the spot with the real token and gate-pass number.
Invoice approval In build The invoice can be reviewed and approved during the outage. The legal GST invoice number is assigned by the office system when the connection returns — so every tax invoice is numbered under central control, exactly as it is today. The driver leaves with the gate pass and weight slip; the tax invoice follows automatically on sync.
Automatic sync In build Everything captured offline uploads in order when the connection returns, with no duplicates and no manual re-entry.

4Always needs the internet

Signing in for the first time Needs internet A session already open continues to work through an outage.
Reports & dashboards Needs internet Sales, stock, P&L, GST reports and analytics are produced centrally.
Payments & ledger Needs internet Receipts, outstanding and account statements.
Government systems Needs internet e-Invoice (IRN) and e-Way Bill are live calls to the GST portal.
Tally sync Needs internet Queued centrally and pushed to Tally automatically once reachable — nothing is lost.
Camera images to cloud Needs internet Live camera view on the plant network continues to work.

5What the operator does during an outage

  1. Carry on weighing. The header shows Offline; the weight display shows the amber Reading scale directly badge.
  2. Do not close or reload the app until the connection is back. (Once the offline database is in place — Section 3 — this caution goes away.)
  3. Record trips as normal. Each one is saved on the PC.
  4. When the connection returns, saved trips upload on their own. The Offline indicator clears once everything has synced.
  5. If a red “needs attention” indicator appears, one saved item could not be accepted — call the office. It has not been lost.
A note on connectivity These measures make the plant resilient to an unreliable link, but they do not replace one. A dual-WAN or 4G-failover router typically removes 30–40 minute outages altogether for a few thousand rupees, and is the single most effective improvement available. We recommend it alongside — not instead of — the work above.
